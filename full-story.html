My Journey

From lines of code to architectural visions.

My name is Bello Oluwanifemi, a 200-level student at Bells University of Technology, Matric No{2024/13766}. My journey didn't start with real estate; it started with curiosity. I've always been fascinated by how things work—whether it's the intricate logic behind a web application or the structural integrity of a skyscraper.

Over the past 4 years, I've honed my skills in web development, specializing in creating immersive digital experiences. I realized that the principles of good software architecture—elegance, efficiency, and user-centricity—parallel those of physical architecture. This realization birthed "Sander House."

I strive to bridge these two worlds, crafting websites that feel like luxury spaces and designing digital solutions that solve real-world problems.
